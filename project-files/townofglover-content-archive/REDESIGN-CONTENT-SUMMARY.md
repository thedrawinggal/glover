# Town of Glover Website Content Summary

## A reorganized guide for website redesign

**Source captured:** July 30, 2026  
**Public website:** https://townofglover.com/  
**Archive status:** 733 public HTML pages, 296,644 extracted words, 1,145 downloaded documents, and 637 downloaded images

> This summary is an additional guide to the complete archive. It does not replace, alter, or remove any captured page, document, image, raw HTML file, inventory, or manifest. Operational details such as names, hours, fees, meeting schedules, and phone numbers should be confirmed before the redesigned website launches.

## How to use this document

The current website mixes permanent municipal information, frequently changing notices, meeting records, project updates, and historical files. This guide reorganizes that content into a clearer resident-centered structure:

1. Essential town information
2. Common resident tasks
3. Town government
4. Meetings and public records
5. Services and public safety
6. Community, recreation, and local organizations
7. Lakes, rivers, flooding, and environmental projects
8. News, announcements, and historical content
9. Recommended structure for the redesigned website
10. Content cleanup and migration priorities

The complete source text remains available in `MASTER-CONTENT.md`, and every captured page remains available as an individual document in `pages/`.

- Fax: 802-525-4115

The current contact list includes the Town Clerk/Treasurer, Select Board, Town Administrator, road crew, listers, constables, justices of the peace, cemetery commissioners, library trustees and librarian, schools, emergency services, health officer, town service officer, and recycling/maintenance coordinator.

# 2. Common Resident Tasks

The redesigned website should foreground tasks rather than requiring residents to understand the town’s organizational structure first.

## Pay a town bill or fee

The website provides online payment paths for:

- Property taxes
- Wastewater payments
- Miscellaneous payments, including grader fees, dog licenses, table and chair rental, and Town Hall rental

**Source:** https://townofglover.com/make-online-payment-to-town-of-glover/

## Research land records and property information

The website directs residents to:

- Use the Cott Systems RecordHub gateway for remote land-record research
- Conduct vault research through the Town Clerk’s Office for a stated fee of $4 per hour, plus copying charges
- Use the Vermont ANR parcel map to view property boundaries and SPAN numbers

The Town Clerk page says the office does not perform title searches or fax records.

**Sources**

- https://townofglover.com/welcome/
- https://townofglover.com/town-clerk/
- https://townofglover.com/glover-town-documents/

## Obtain permits or building guidance

The Town Clerk page states:

- Glover does not have municipal zoning
- Work around the lakes may be subject to Vermont Agency of Natural Resources requirements
- A driveway permit is required
- Driveway-permit applications are available from the Town Clerk’s Office

These statements should be reviewed and expanded into a dedicated “Building, Property, and Permits” page that clearly distinguishes town, state, lake, wastewater, flood-hazard, and driveway requirements.

**Source:** https://townofglover.com/town-clerk/

## License a dog

The current page states that dog licenses are due April 1 and that residents should bring:

- A spay/neuter certificate, when applicable
- A current rabies vaccination certificate

The page also displays license fees and higher post-April 1 fees. Because fees can change, the redesigned page should store them in a clearly dated fee schedule rather than burying them in general office copy.

**Source:** https://townofglover.com/town-clerk/

## Register or renew a motor vehicle

The Town Clerk page describes limited motor-vehicle registration renewal services for the current month and two prior months. It specifies payment to the Vermont Department of Motor Vehicles and a separate town charge.

This information should be verified and presented as a short eligibility and payment checklist.

**Source:** https://townofglover.com/town-clerk/

## Rent town facilities, tables, or chairs

The contact and payment pages reference:

- Town Hall rental
- Table and chair rental
- The Town Clerk’s Office as the contact
- Online miscellaneous-payment capability

The redesigned site should consolidate availability, policies, fees, application forms, capacity, accessibility, and payment into one “Town Hall and Equipment Rentals” page.

**Sources**

- https://townofglover.com/contact-list/

# 10. Content Cleanup and Migration Priorities

## Priority 1: Confirm high-impact operational information

Verify before launch:

- Staff and elected-official names
- Phone numbers and email addresses
- Office and facility hours
- Meeting schedules
- Fees and payment instructions
- Emergency and non-emergency contacts
- Recycling and disposal rules
- Permit and state-agency guidance

## Priority 2: Consolidate duplicated content

Important duplication currently includes:

- Home page and Welcome page
- Select Board landing page and Select Board category archive
- Cemetery Commission landing page and category archive
- Recycling information on the Welcome, Services, and Recycling Center pages
- Contact details repeated across department pages and the contact list
- Meeting records repeated in posts, yearly pages, committee pages, and Google Drive

The redesigned site should store each fact once and display it wherever needed.

## Priority 3: Resolve dated or contradictory statements

Examples found in the captured content:

- The Welcome page contains pandemic-era mask language
- The park page says pickleball is “coming in 2025”
- Recycling details appear in several places and are not equally current
- Project pages mix older updates with current information
- Some contact information is incomplete or inconsistently formatted
- A Select Board email appears once without a complete domain

Every permanent page should display a “last reviewed” date and an accountable content owner.

## Priority 4: Improve readability

Common issues in the current content include:

- Long undifferentiated paragraphs
- Meeting dates run together without spacing
- Links represented only by dates or “click here”
- Inconsistent capitalization and punctuation
- Typos in labels and names
- Unclear draft/approved document status
- Page content mixed with social-sharing and theme text
- Empty or nearly empty landing pages that rely entirely on embeds or downloads

## Priority 5: Repair broken references

The live capture found:

- One linked historical page returning 404
- Ten historical downloadable documents returning 404
- Four image references returning 404

All broken URLs and their referring pages are listed in `BROKEN-OR-UNAVAILABLE-LINKS.md`.

## Priority 6: Preserve legal and historical records

Historical records should not be discarded merely because they are old or poorly organized. They should be:

- Retained in their original format
- Assigned metadata
- Connected to the responsible board or topic
- Searchable by year and record type
- Clearly separated from current instructions
- Marked as superseded when applicable
