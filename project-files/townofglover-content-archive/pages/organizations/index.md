### Non-Profit Societies of Glover

Glover Historical Society P.O. Box 208 Glover, VT 05839 www.gloverhistoricalsociety.org gloverhistory@yahoo.com Society dedicated to the preservation of local history.

Lake Parker Association P.O. Box 40 West Glover, VT 05875 www.lakeparker.org The LPA is an active non-profit citizens group concerned with protecting the ecological health of Lake Parker and its watershed.
