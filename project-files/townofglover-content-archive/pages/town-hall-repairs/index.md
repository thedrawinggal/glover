## Page content

Sending out a thank you to all who came out to help remove all furniture from the town hall this past Saturday morning.

Special thank you to those who leant us the trailers for three weeks in order to put them in storage.

The bathroom has been repaired including the floor and new plumbing. The ceilings in the main hall room have been repaired and painted, all the walls are painted, woodwork has been sanded, stained and polyed and today the sanding of all the wood flooring begins. Yet to do is the painting in the bathroom and new spot lights for the stage and of course the outside paintings and repairs.

Thanks to all who have been a guidance and a help.

Theresa
