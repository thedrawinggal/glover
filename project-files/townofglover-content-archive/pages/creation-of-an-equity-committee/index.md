## Page content

At its October 14th regular meeting, the Glover Select Board approved the creation of an Equity Committee charged with the task recommending a Declaration of Inclusion for the board to adopt and with making recommendations to the board for how it can promote and sustain equity, inclusion and diversity in the town of Glover. If you are a resident of Glover, committed to these values and want to serve on this committee, e-mail bcarrollgloversb@comcast.net.
