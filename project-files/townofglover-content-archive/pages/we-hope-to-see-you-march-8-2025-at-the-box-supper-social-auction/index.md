## Page content

We have auctioneer Justin Lander to entertain us. Pack an embellished box, basket or pail with your specialty dinner foods for 2 and drink of your choice. Come join the fun! Your box will be auctioned off to the highest bidder. Everyone will gather at a Farmer’s Table to share their meal and conversation. This event will be at the Glover Town Hall, from 6 p.m. to 9 p.m. We plan to have music by Jeremy Harple and we invite you to bring an instrument to put together a “Kitchen Junket Band.”

All proceeds support the Shadow Lake Dam Fund.

RSVP or questions at 802 624-3463, 802 525-7199, theresa@townofglover.com, or 802 525-6227. http://shadowlakedam.org or http://townofglover.com – Send me an email I will be happy to send you the story of a box supper event I went to as a child.

Hope to see you there!
