Auction Items Needed!

Auction – May 10th, join us for potluck at 5 p.m. auction starts at 6 p.m.

Do you own a business and could you donate a gift certificate to a worthy cause? Do you have talent, are you an artist and have art to donate? Do you grow beautiful plants? Is it time to purge items you no longer need or use? We are looking for good, useful items for our SLD Auction. Maybe you could donate a cord of wood, or a truck load of stone, a gift basket of home-raised meat items, they would make a good auction item! EGGS! – We all love fresh eggs! Be creative with your items – make a gift basket of home-baked goods. Do you do yearly canning? – Create a gift basket of jellies, jams or pickles. Doughnuts, fresh baked bread, pies and cookies all make great items to auction off. Are you a business that could donate gift certificates? Have you been looking for a home for those used furnishings? – We can help.Drop off items on May 9th from 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. at the Glover Town Hall or Mail your Gift Certificates to Town of Glover, 51 Bean Hill Road, Glover, VT 05839 Att: Shadow Lake Dam.

Please call for questions or to inform us of your donation at 802 525-7199 or email: theresa@townofglover.comHelp Save The Shadow Lake Dam! shadowlakedam.org
