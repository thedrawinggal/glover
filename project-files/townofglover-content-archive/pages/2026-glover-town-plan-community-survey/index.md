## Page content

Every eight years, our community comes together to rethink and refresh the Glover Town Plan. The current plan, last reviewed in 2018, is due for completion by the end of 2026. Why is this so important? A clear and well-structured Town Plan is a practical roadmap for shaping our collective future. It also opens the door to valuable resources—by articulating our goals and concerns, Glover becomes more likely to receive grants and assistance to help bring these visions to life.

Crafting a Town Plan involves taking an honest look at our hometown—honoring our history, pinpointing our strengths and areas that need attention, and envisioning the possibilities ahead. This survey is a vital first step in that process. Your input will help identify priorities, refine objectives, and drive meaningful projects in coordination with neighboring towns and regional plans.

To ensure everyone has an opportunity to participate, the survey will remain open through the end of March 2025.

If you have questions, you may contact a member of the Selectboard or a member of the Planning Commission. Please go to the Town of Glover website for contact information.

As a thank-you, all participants who provide contact information will be entered into a raffle for a chance to win one of two $25 Currier’s/C&C gift cards or a $25 Parker Pie gift card. Your perspective matters—let’s shape Glover’s future together!
