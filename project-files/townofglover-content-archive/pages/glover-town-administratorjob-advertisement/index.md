Position: Glover Town Administrator

The town of Glover seeks a Town Administrator, a newly created position to support both functions of the Town Clerk/Treasurer’s office and the Town’s Select Board. This position will report to the Town Clerk/Treasurer and the Chair of the Glover Select Board. Along with a good sense of humor, ideal candidate should be well organized, possess strong communication skills, have basic experience and knowledge related to municipal government, board policy, accounting practices, tax procurement, land recordings, social media, general elections and public meetings management, among other general duties. Experience with the municipal accounting program, NEMRC, is desired.

Interested parties should contact brian@townofglover.com for an application for submission along with a resume and (3) letters of reference to either Cindy@townofglover.com or David@townofglover.com. Glover is an equal opportunity employer.
