## Page content

Due to flood damage, tonight’s Select Board Meeting will take place in the back room at the Glover Fire Department, beginning at 6:45. This will be an in-person meeting only, with no hybrid option to attend online. We regret any inconvenience that this might cause, but made this decision out of concern for the health and safety of our community members.
