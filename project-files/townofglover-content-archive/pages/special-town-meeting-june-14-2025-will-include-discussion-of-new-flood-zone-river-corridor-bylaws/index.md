## Page content

The Glover Planning Commission and Select board are considering mandatory updates to the Glover Flood Hazard Area Regulation and the possible addition of a River Corridor Bylaw.There will be time at the end of the SPECIAL TOWN MEETING on SATURDAY, June 14, 2025 for discussion and questions regarding these changes.Please attend the June 14, 10:00 AM Special Town Meeting, at the TOWN HALLto learn more about these changes.Reminder: the 1991 Flood Area Regulation is in effect. Any development in the Flood Hazard Area requires a permit. Permit applications are available at the Town Office.
