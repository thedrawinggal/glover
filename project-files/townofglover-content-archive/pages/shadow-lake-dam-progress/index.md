## Page content

Weston & Sampson begin their analysis on the Shadow Lake Dam starting the week of, (12/9/24). Please be respectful with the workers as they do their work and do not enter the property while they are there.

You can find more information at https://shadowlakedam.org/ including video and photos as they come available. Contact Theresa Perron with questions: 802 525-7199.
