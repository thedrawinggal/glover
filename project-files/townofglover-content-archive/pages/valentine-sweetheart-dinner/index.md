HURRY AND PLACE YOUR ORDER

February 14, 2025 – Glover Town Hall Valentine Sweetheart Dinner $25 by Alfreda’s Catering 5 p.m.Dam Dixieland Jazz Band – Starting at 6:30 p.m.Your Choice: Pork Tenderloin, Baked Stuffed Chicken BreastPotatoes, Maple Baked Winter Squash, Green BeansRoll, Dessert, Dine in Our Romantic Setting w/Music by Jim CurrierReservations only: Theresa@townofglover.com Include choice of meal, Name, Address, Number, Email.Reserved Town Hall Seatings 5 p.m. – 7 p.m. Takeout Pre-Ordered a Must.Delivery Tip AppreciatedQuestions, Reservations, or Volunteer Call: 802 525-7199 or 802 624-3463.All Proceeds for Shadow Lake Dam Fund. Donations Welcomed.Send Dinner Order & Check to Town of Glover, 51 Bean Hill Road, Glover, VT 05839 or dropyour check at the Town Clerk’s Office & memo: Attention: SLD Valentine Dinner.
