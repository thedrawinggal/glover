## Page content

The University of Vermont is conducting a study to better understand mobility and housing needs of those affected by the 2023 summer floods. We are interested in hearing from people 18 and older whose housing, belongings or property were affected. The survey is available here:

go.uvm.edu/flood

Study participants will be entered into a raffle for a chance to win one of ten $50 gift cards to thank them for their time.

Thank you for your consideration.

Please contact the following people for more information:

Sarah Grajdura, PhDPostdoctoral AssociateDepartment of Civil and Environmental Engineering,The University of Vermontsarah.grajdura@uvm.edu

Dana Rowangould, PhDAssistant ProfessorDepartment of Civil and Environmental Engineering,The University of Vermontdana.rowangould@uvm.edu
