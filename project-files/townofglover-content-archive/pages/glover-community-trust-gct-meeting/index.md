## Page content

Wednesday, August 13, 6:30 – 7:30 p.m.

Glover Town Hall

GCT is a group of community residents coming together to consider available properties to purchase, renovate, and create housing opportunities, through grants and fundraising.

Other possibilities for these properties are an option. GCT is open to ideas and conversations.

Come join this small group and help build a safe, vibrant, productive community for all.

Interested? Need more info? Email Barb Delzio at barbdelzio@gmail.com
