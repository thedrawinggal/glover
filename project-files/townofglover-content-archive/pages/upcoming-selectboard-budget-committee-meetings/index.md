## Page content

Please join us on Thursday, February 5, 2026, and Thursday, February 19, 2026, from 6:00pm – 8:00pm at the Glover Town Hall to discuss the 2026 Proposed Budget and Presentation in anticipation for Town Meeting. An agenda will be posted on the website, 3 physical locations, and Zoom will be available. Proposed budgets will be available, along with notes and clarifying explanations. Thank you!
