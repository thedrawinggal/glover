## Page content

Are you a student looking to earn extra money this summer? Or a retiree with an interest in preserving our ecosystems? The Glover Recycling Center seeks a part-attendant, starting in August. You’ll stay busy, meet and greet your community and make a difference with very little stress, Contact brian@townofglover.com for details.

Brian F. Carroll, Town Administrator Town of Glover, Vermont 802.673.7149
