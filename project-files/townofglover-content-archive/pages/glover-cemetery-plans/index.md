## Page content

The Glover Cemetery Commission is offering two new burial options at the Westlook II Cemetery, hopefully by this fall: (1) a green/natural burial section, and (2) a memorial garden. On Wednesday, June 29 at 7 PM at the Glover Town Hall, the commission will be sharing the plans for both options. All are invited to attend.
