## Page content

Please be aware that the lakes and ponds in Glover have not been tested yet, and swimming is at your own risk. We are currently seeking further guidance. We hope to provide further information soon. We are testing now, and will be testing again on Monday after the next upcoming rainstorms, but again, do not advise swimming at this time.
