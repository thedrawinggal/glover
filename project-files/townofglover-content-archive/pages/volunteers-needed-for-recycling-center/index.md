HELP NEEDED

We are creating a list of volunteers to help with the Recycling Center during the regular hours:

Wednesday, 12 a.m. to 3 p.m. and Saturdays 9 a.m. to 12 p.m.

We will rotate the list to avoid using the same persons twice in the same month.

Please call Theresa Perron to be put on that list at 802 525-6227.
