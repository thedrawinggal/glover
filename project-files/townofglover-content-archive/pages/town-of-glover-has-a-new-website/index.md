## Page content

Come visit to stay informed about town business and happenings. The new site offers better access, less clicking, and more updated information. In particular, you can now view Recordings of Select Board Meetings and join Upcoming Zoom Meetings. You can also share all posts from the site as well as subscribe in the footer via email to all posts, as well as follow posts on Facebook.
