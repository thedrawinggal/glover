## Page content

The flood waters have receded from the basement of the Town Hall have receded, leaving the floor and many low-placed its covered in mud. There is still a lot of moisture in the basement, and we need more fans and dehumidifiers. If anyone has any to loan, please bring them to the Town Hall as soon as you can. We could also use help cleaning the tables that were in the basement and loading them into the elevator to move them upstairs. Once we have a dumpster, we may need help removing other items from the basement as well. If you can help, email leanne@townofglover.com or just come on down to the Town Hall today.
