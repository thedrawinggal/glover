### Glover Town Clerks Office

phone (802) 525-6227 fax (802) 525-4115 HOURS: 7:00 am- 5:00 pm Monday – Thursday Closed Fridays, Saturdays, Sundays, and Holidays Cindy Epinette, Clerk/Treasurer Email

Town Clerk’s Office Services

Zoning: There is no zoning in Glover. If you are building or remodeling around the lakes, you need to contact ANR to learn about their requirements.

Driveway permit is required. Applications are available at the town clerk’s office.

Vault time, $4.00 per hour for research, there is a charge for copies, we do not fax and we do not do title searches.

Motor Vehicle registration renewal for the current month and two months prior, must be check or money order payable to Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV) for the amount due, $3.00 charge for the town cash or check.

Select Board Meetings the 2nd and 4th Thursday of the month at 6:30 PM at the Town Hall. To get on the agenda call the Town Clerk’s Office, All meetings are open to the public.

Dog licenses due April 1st, bring certificates for spayed females and neutered males and rabies vaccination.

Prices: Male & Female $15.00 Spayed/Neutered $ 11.00 After April First: Male & Female $19.00 Spayed/Neutered $ 13.00

Town of Glover Dog and Wolf-hybrid ordinance

Glover Cemeteries French Cemetery -full Andersonville Cemetery – full West Glover Cemetery – full Westlook Cemetery – lots available on right side of cemetery next to Route 122 Westlook II Cemetery
