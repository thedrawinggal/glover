## Page content

The Glover Select Board would like to express disappointment and apologies to those who attempted to access the February meetings remotely. Due to some technical difficulties, we were unable to make the critical connections. Rest assured, we have been working on solving the glitches and will be prepared to offer Town Meeting access under a remote/hybrid format on March 7th. Please be aware that, if you do choose to access remotely, you will not be eligible for voting on the presented articles.
