#### Rubbish Removal

Bagged household trash can be brought to Glover Recycling. Charges per bag are:$3 – Kitchen size bag$5 – 30 gallon size bag$8 – Contractor size bag

Our dumpster is limited in size, so PLEASE, if you are cleaning out and have a pickup load of trash take it directly to the landfill in Coventry.

Residents should make their own arrangements with private haulers, or they may take their garbage to Waste U.S.A. in Coventry.Following is a list of private garbage haulers operating in Glover.

Chayer’s Rubbish Removal – 802 673-3376Call for rates. Dave’s Rubbish – 802-334-5757Residential and commercial. Weekly and bi-weekly pickups available, call for rates. Available are Roll-offs, dumpsters, and 96-gallon carts for residential service.

Myers – 800-981-5213 and Local Number 802-748-3973. Call for residential and commercial rates.

Casella Waste Management – 800-639-3083 or direct line at 888 485-1469Rate depends on frequency and volume. Recyclable materials can be picked up.

ALL Metals – 802 472-5100 Scrap metal and construction debris and roll offs.
