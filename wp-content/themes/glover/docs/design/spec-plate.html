Components

Rendered live from the system's own values. Hover and tab through them: hover, focus and pressed states are real.

Primary Button

The one main action of a page, form or dialog. Forest Dark pill, Cream label.

Accent Button

Starts a service or a time-limited task where a Primary nearby would compete. Orange pill.

Secondary Button

A helpful action beside a Primary, or a standalone action that isn't the page's main job. Teal pill.

Outline Button

Low emphasis: cancel, back, view. 2px Forest Dark stroke, no fill.

Buttons on Forest

In a banner or forest panel, the first action is Teal and any others are Cream outlines. The focus ring turns Orange.

The Rules

A screen that breaks one of these is a defect, not a variation.

The AA Rule

Every public page meets WCAG 2.1 AA. Text is at least 4.5:1, or 3:1 at 24px and larger. Control borders, focus rings and meaningful icons reach 3:1. Additional layers of accessibility are permitted using a custom accessibility widget, never in place of meeting AA.

The Signal Rule

Fresh and Orange are never text or icons on a light ground. On light grounds they are fills behind Forest Dark or Ink text. As text, they appear only on Forest and Forest Dark.

The Focus Rule

Every control shows a 3px ring with a 2 to 3px offset when focused from the keyboard: Fresh Dark on light grounds, Orange on Forest grounds. No rule removes it.

The Link Rule

Links are underlined. On light grounds they are Link Green; on Forest grounds, Cream or Fresh, and the banner's breadcrumb is Orange. Color alone never marks a link.

The One Container Rule

Every page uses $CONTAINER-WIDTH (82rem) and $GUTTER (20px on phones up to 80px). No page or feature sets its own max width.

The Pill Rule

Every button, chip, tag and search bar is a pill. Fields are 8px, cards and notices 12px, feature panels 20px, badges 4px. Nothing else is rounded any other way.

The Serif Rule

Young Serif sets headings only, at weight 400, never bold. Figtree sets everything else. A third face is a defect.

The One Primary Rule

A view has one Primary button. A second main action is Accent or Secondary, and a link that only goes somewhere is a Text Link.

The Written Status Rule

Status is a word: Published, Pending Review, Draft, Returned. Color reinforces the word and never replaces it. Every field has a visible label.

The Title Case Rule

Titles, headings, labels and buttons are Title Case. Body and help text are sentence case. Buttons start with a verb.

The Plain Words Rule

Interface copy gives a person what they need to act and never describes what the system did. No em dashes. Errors name the problem and the fix.

The Flat Rule

Fills and lines separate surfaces. The only shadow is the dialog's, because a dialog floats over the page and leaves.

Do and Don't

Do

  • Do keep every color in _colors.scss and reference it through --color-*; partials never carry a raw hex.
  • Do keep the public site light with forest bookends: Forest Dark header and footer, a Forest banner, cream between.
  • Do use Teal for secondary actions, notices, selected states and badges, with Forest Dark text.
  • Do use Orange for Accent buttons, attention notices, search matches, labels and breadcrumbs on forest.
  • Do set headings in Young Serif 400 and let size and color carry the hierarchy.
  • Do use placeholders like [Town Clerk email] until the town supplies real details.

Don't

  • Don't put Fresh or Orange text on cream, white, Surface or Teal.
  • Don't bold Young Serif, set body text in it, or add a third typeface.
  • Don't square off a button or chip, add a shadow to anything but a dialog, or set a page its own max width.
  • Don't remove a focus ring, or mark a link or a status with color alone.
  • Don't use Gold, the old blue focus color, Chip or Highlight; they are not in the system.
  • Don't invent town facts: addresses, phone numbers, meeting times or names.
